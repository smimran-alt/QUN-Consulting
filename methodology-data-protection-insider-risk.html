HOW OUR METHODOLOGY WORKS

From the challenge to a clear path forward.

Our Data Protection & Insider Risk methodology is structured, collaborative and tailored. It combines business context, relevant technical or governance evidence, stakeholder decisions and practical follow-through.

The sequence may be adapted to engagement scope. Risk oversight and stakeholder alignment continue throughout.

What we do at each stage

  • Discover data: Understand where sensitive information lives and how it moves.
  • Classify: Classify information against business value and obligations.
  • Protect: Design proportionate DLP, access and sharing safeguards.
  • Monitor: Monitor relevant events with transparency and approved governance.
  • Investigate: Apply defined escalation, privacy and evidence-handling procedures.
  • Improve: Tune controls and training using findings and operational feedback.

What the client receives

  • Data risk and control inventory
  • DLP and insider-risk use-case catalogue
  • Governance, investigation workflow and phased rollout plan

Where this helps

Use these activities to turn findings into accountable, proportionate security improvements.
