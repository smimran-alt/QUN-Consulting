HOW OUR METHODOLOGY WORKS

From the challenge to a clear path forward.

Our ISO 42001 AI Governance methodology is structured, collaborative and tailored. It combines business context, relevant technical or governance evidence, stakeholder decisions and practical follow-through.

02Governance context

WHAT YOU RECEIVEAccountable AI adoption

The sequence may be adapted to engagement scope. Risk oversight and stakeholder alignment continue throughout.

What we do at each stage

  • AI inventory: Catalog AI systems, providers, business uses and decision owners.
  • Governance context: Set policy, responsibilities and stakeholder expectations.
  • Impact and risk: Evaluate safety, security, privacy, bias and business impact risks.
  • Control design: Define lifecycle safeguards, human oversight and supplier controls.
  • Assurance review: Review controls and evidence against stated governance requirements.
  • Ongoing oversight: Monitor model, data and use-case changes and respond to emerging risks.

What the client receives

  • AI system inventory and classification
  • AI governance framework and risk register
  • ISO/IEC 42001 readiness roadmap and evidence plan
