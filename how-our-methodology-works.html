PHASE 01 / 06

Discover

Define the problem before selecting the solution.

We establish the business, technology and regulatory context of the engagement. We listen to key stakeholders, identify critical services and information assets, agree the boundaries of the review, and establish how success will be measured.

How we work

  • Engage sponsors, control owners and operational stakeholders
  • Identify business services, data, technology and dependencies
  • Understand risk appetite, obligations and relevant standards
  • Agree scope, assumptions, constraints and decision gates
TYPICAL CLIENT DELIVERABLES
  • Engagement charter and statement of scope
  • Stakeholder and critical-asset map
  • Evidence request and delivery plan
Decision at this stage: What matters most, and what must this engagement achieve?

PHASE 02 / 06

Assess

Understand exposure through evidence—not assumption.

We examine the current state through interviews, walkthroughs, technical evidence, control testing and risk analysis appropriate to the service. Findings are evaluated for business impact, likelihood, maturity and compliance relevance.

How we work

  • Review controls, policies, architecture and available evidence
  • Identify threats, vulnerabilities, dependencies and control gaps
  • Assess risk and prioritize material exposures
  • Validate observations with owners before finalizing findings
TYPICAL CLIENT DELIVERABLES
  • Risk-ranked findings register
  • Current-state and maturity assessment
  • Evidence-based gap analysis
Decision at this stage: Which weaknesses deserve attention first?
QUN CONSULTING / 02 — 06
PHASE 03 / 06

Design

Turn findings into a realistic, prioritized plan.

We collaborate with stakeholders to develop a proportionate target state. Recommendations balance risk reduction, business usability, technology constraints, cost and ownership. Each proposed improvement is translated into an actionable roadmap.

How we work

  • Define target capabilities and security principles
  • Recommend safeguards, processes, ownership and metrics
  • Sequence initiatives by risk reduction, effort and dependencies
  • Agree acceptance criteria, governance and milestones
TYPICAL CLIENT DELIVERABLES
  • Target-state architecture or operating model
  • Prioritized remediation and transformation roadmap
  • Control design and implementation requirements
Decision at this stage: What should change, in what order, and why?
QUN CONSULTING / 03 — 06
PHASE 04 / 06

Implement

Move from recommendation to operational change.

We support delivery of approved safeguards, governance practices and operating procedures. The work is coordinated with accountable teams to encourage adoption, maintain business continuity and ensure that changes can be operated sustainably.

How we work

  • Assign responsibilities, milestones and dependencies
  • Support deployment of controls and process improvements
  • Prepare policies, runbooks and operational documentation
  • Enable users, teams and management through knowledge transfer
TYPICAL CLIENT DELIVERABLES
  • Implemented controls and procedures
  • Ownership and operating documentation
  • Delivery status, decisions and issue log
Decision at this stage: Are the agreed improvements in place and owned?
QUN CONSULTING / 04 — 06
PHASE 05 / 06

Validate

Verify that change delivers the intended outcome.

We independently or collaboratively test the implemented improvements against agreed acceptance criteria. Residual risks and unresolved gaps are recorded clearly, with evidence that decision-makers can use for assurance, audit and governance.

How we work

  • Test control operation, coverage and performance
  • Review implementation evidence and remediation results
  • Confirm exceptions and residual risks with risk owners
  • Present assurance findings and prioritized follow-up actions
TYPICAL CLIENT DELIVERABLES
  • Control validation and assurance report
  • Evidence pack and closure register
  • Residual-risk and exception decisions
Decision at this stage: Do the changes work, and can we prove it?
QUN CONSULTING / 05 — 06
PHASE 06 / 06

Optimize

Make security progress measurable and repeatable.

Security needs evolve as technology, business priorities and threats change. We establish performance monitoring and review routines that enable the organization to detect drift, learn from incidents and adapt controls over time.

How we work

  • Define KPIs, KRIs and review cadence
  • Track control performance and new or emerging risks
  • Capture lessons learned and improvement opportunities
  • Refresh the roadmap, governance and assurance priorities
TYPICAL CLIENT DELIVERABLES
  • Security improvement backlog and roadmap refresh
  • Performance scorecard and review cadence
  • Continuous improvement and ownership plan
Decision at this stage: How will improvement continue after the engagement?

WHAT UNDERPINS EVERY ENGAGEMENT

Four principles guide our work.

Business-aligned

Scope and recommendations are grounded in organizational priorities and the value at risk.

Risk-based

Effort is directed toward the most material threats, exposures and control weaknesses.

Standards-informed

Recognized frameworks, including ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 42001 and NIST CSF where relevant, inform the approach.

Evidence-led

Findings, decisions and outcomes are supported by demonstrable evidence and practical measures.
