HOW OUR METHODOLOGY WORKS

From the challenge to a clear path forward.

Our Penetration Testing methodology is structured, collaborative and tailored. It combines business context, relevant technical or governance evidence, stakeholder decisions and practical follow-through.

01Authorize & scope
02Threat-led planning
03Controlled testing
04Validate impact
05Report findings
06Retest

The sequence may be adapted to engagement scope. Risk oversight and stakeholder alignment continue throughout.

What we do at each stage

  • Authorize & scope: Obtain explicit authorization, rules of engagement and safety constraints.
  • Threat-led planning: Prioritize realistic attack surfaces and agreed objectives.
  • Controlled testing: Conduct controlled assessments using appropriate manual and automated methods.
  • Validate impact: Confirm findings safely and assess plausible business impact.
  • Report findings: Deliver prioritized findings and actionable remediation recommendations.
  • Retest: Recheck agreed fixes and document residual exposure.

What the client receives

  • Authorized rules of engagement and test plan
  • Evidence-backed findings with risk severity
  • Remediation guidance and retest summary

Where this helps

Use these activities to turn findings into accountable, proportionate security improvements.
