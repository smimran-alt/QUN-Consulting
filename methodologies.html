HOW THE METHODOLOGY WORKS

Clear decisions. Tangible deliverables. Lasting assurance.

Our methodology is a flexible delivery framework, not a rigid checklist. Each stage builds on the previous one, with governance, stakeholder involvement and risk-based decisions throughout. The depth of work is tailored to the client's scope and context.

01 ↗

Discover

Understand the organization before recommending a solution.

We engage stakeholders, confirm business and regulatory priorities, identify critical services and define the scope and success criteria.

Typical deliverablesEngagement scope, stakeholder map and agreed assessment plan.
02 ↗

Assess

Establish a defensible, evidence-based view of the current state.

We examine risks, threats, existing controls, process maturity and relevant standards to identify material gaps and opportunities.

Typical deliverablesAssessment findings, risk profile and prioritized gap register.
03 ↗

Design

Translate findings into decisions and an achievable target state.

We define proportionate safeguards, ownership, policies, operating processes and a phased roadmap aligned with available resources.

Typical deliverablesTarget-state design, control recommendations and implementation roadmap.
04 ↗

Implement

Turn recommendations into operational improvements.

We support the rollout of agreed controls, processes, governance mechanisms and stakeholder enablement, with responsibilities clearly assigned.

Typical deliverablesImplemented actions, updated documentation and accountable owners.
05 ↗

Validate

Test whether the improvements work as intended.

We review evidence, test control effectiveness, confirm remediation and report remaining risks clearly to decision-makers.

Typical deliverablesValidation evidence, assurance report and residual-risk decisions.
06 ↗

Optimize

Keep the program effective as priorities and threats change.

We define measures, track residual risks, refresh controls and use lessons learned to inform ongoing improvements.

Typical deliverablesPerformance measures, improvement backlog and review cadence.
CONTINUOUS OVERSIGHT

Governance is embedded across every stage.

Risks, dependencies, decisions and outcomes are reviewed as the engagement progresses. Where appropriate, outputs are aligned to recognized frameworks such as ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 42001, NIST CSF and COBIT.
