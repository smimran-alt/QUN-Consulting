HOW OUR METHODOLOGY WORKS

From the challenge to a clear path forward.

Our ISO 27001 ISMS methodology is structured, collaborative and tailored. It combines business context, relevant technical or governance evidence, stakeholder decisions and practical follow-through.

02Understand context
03Assess risks
04Design controls
05Audit readiness
06Improve
WHAT WE EXAMINERelevant controls, evidence and business exposure
HOW WE PRIORITIZEMaterial risk, operating constraints and ownership
WHAT YOU RECEIVERepeatable security governance

The sequence may be adapted to engagement scope. Risk oversight and stakeholder alignment continue throughout.

What we do at each stage

  • Define scope: Agree organizational boundaries, critical assets and interested parties.
  • Understand context: Understand regulatory obligations, policies, roles and strategic objectives.
  • Assess risks: Assess information security risks using a repeatable method.
  • Design controls: Select proportionate risk treatment and document the Statement of Applicability.
  • Audit readiness: Review evidence, run internal audit activities and prepare management review.
  • Improve: Address gaps and maintain continual improvement practices.

What the client receives

  • ISMS scope and governance documents
  • Risk assessment, treatment plan and Statement of Applicability
  • Gap findings, corrective action plan and readiness report

Where this helps

Use these activities to turn findings into accountable, proportionate security improvements.
